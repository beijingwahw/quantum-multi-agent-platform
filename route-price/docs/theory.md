# Theory — route and price for the two OPEN rows

The atlas (`../bqp-map`) enforces a discipline the visitor's letter made law: OPEN rows quote no numbers. What an OPEN row owes instead is a route with falsifiers and prices with derivations. This document records the anchors the dossiers stand on; the executable layer (`src/`) re-derives every figure the price lines quote.

## The two dossiers

- **D1 — dtc-clock.** The "zero-energy eternal beat" splits into three truths: equilibrium no-beat (WO15, closed as impossible), driven beat (WIL12 proposed, MI22 demonstrated at NISQ scale, paid by the drive every period), and clocking general computation (executed at the model layer by `../dtc-clock` — milestones M3/M4 carry execution records since v0.2.0, each backed by this repo's own cross-check witnesses W-D/W-E). The prices: kT ln2 per irreversible bit (two-path in W-A), metered readout growing superlinearly with depth, the in-model tariff table re-priced on this repo's own netlist (W-E), and the deliberately unquoted hardware power cord — its boundary sharpened: every in-model face is metered, the hardware joules are not computable here (ERS17/VHM26/NS25 price timekeeping's thermodynamics in theory; the drive's dissipated energy is an experiment's number).
- **D2 — choice-primitive.** The nearest legal relative of `choose` is the controlled-unitary — coherent branching is textbook-legal; what fails is broadcasting the condition (WZ82, executed as the rephasing-covariance gap in W-B) and defaulting stability (invariance is engineered, W-B: exact for compiled targets, near the Haar mean for random programs). SEL04's quantum-data-classical-control design is the boundary the route starts from, not an oversight it corrects. The certification toll is the epoch-3 ledger re-invoiced (W-C). The Noether question is answered at the mechanism-design layer since `../dsic-noether` shipped the Groves gauge group and its welfare-gap charge (discrete bitwise + continuum coefficient-wise); milestone M4 carries the execution record with cross-check W-F, and the LANGUAGE-level charge — the one that would guard `choose` semantics — remains unidentified and unpriced.

## The executions and their cross-checks (v0.2.0)

By this repo's own law it cannot settle anything; what it can do since v0.2.0 is verify its neighbors' receipts. A milestone may claim an execution only with three things at once (law R7): the sibling repo (on disk), the sibling's certificate id, and a witness of THIS repo that re-derives the cited numbers at toy scale with this repo's own kernels — no sibling code is ever imported.

- **W-D — the beat-register cross-check (backs D1-M3).** The driven echo F = e^{-iθ ΣX}·e^{-i H_zz} rebuilt from this repo's linalg: at θ = π/2, h = 0 the identity F† Z_i F = −Z_i holds at machine precision for any couplings (worst 2.22e-16, n = 4, five random-J trials) because every ZZ pair is flip-even; the trajectory alternates m(k) = (−1)^k (worst 1.11e-15, k ≤ 12); the register returns exactly after every 2k periods (the subharmonic face). The compiled cargo: this repo's own TOFFOLI+CNOT 2×2-bit multiplier netlist (11 gates, 13 wires) — 16/16 integer-exact, a bijection of the full 8192-state wire cube, bitwise self-reset under reversal, 5 garbage wires (the tariff's as-built row).
- **W-E — the energy-certificate cross-check (backs D1-M4).** Zero net work on the ideal beat (orbit energy flat to 2.66e-15 at n = 6, J = 1.3); the detuned first period's closed forms ⟨H_zz⟩₁ = −J(n−1)cos²2δ and W₀ = J(n−1)sin²2δ re-derived numerically (W₀ = 0.256552, the sibling's 0.2566); the isolated qubit's stroboscope follows |⟨Z⟩_k| = |cos 2kδ| exactly — coherent oscillation with revivals, an honest correction to the geometric envelope |cos2δ|^k sometimes quoted for it; and the tariff table re-priced on this repo's own machine: Bennett 0 < as-built 5 < irreversible 9 < FK spectral 43.02 kT·ln2 units, with joule faces 1.4355e-20 J (300 K) and 8.6129e-25 J (10 mK) from this repo's own ln2 quadrature.
- **W-F — the welfare-gap charge cross-check (backs D2-M4).** On the quadratic divisible-good family (v = θa − a²/2, Σa = 1), in exact BigInt rationals (`src/kernel/exact.ts` — floats banned from that file): the welfare gap equals the closed-form charge −(n−1)(s−t)²/(2n) bitwise (72 probes, n ∈ {2,3}); the charge is zero exactly at truth; two gauges shift every Groves payment by exactly h − h′ and leave every deviation gain bitwise unchanged; and the off-gauge payment p + εs buys a profitable deviation worth exactly ε²n/(2(n−1)) at s* = t − εn/(n−1) (ε = 1/7: worth 1/49 at n = 2, 3/196 at n = 3). The tests carry the independent float path on binary-exact grids — the two-path law applied to the charge.
