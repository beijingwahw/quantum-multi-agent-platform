# Theory — route and price for the two OPEN rows

The atlas (`../bqp-map`) enforces a discipline the visitor's letter made law: OPEN rows quote no numbers. What an OPEN row owes instead is a route with falsifiers and prices with derivations. This document records the anchors the dossiers stand on; the executable layer (`src/`) re-derives every figure the price lines quote.

## The two dossiers

- **D1 — dtc-clock.** The "zero-energy eternal beat" splits into three truths: equilibrium no-beat (WO15, closed as impossible), driven beat (WIL12 proposed, MI22 demonstrated at NISQ scale, paid by the drive every period), and clocking general computation (uncertified; the route runs through phase-locking a register and an energy certificate against the Landauer floor). The prices: kT ln2 per irreversible bit (two-path in W-A), metered readout growing superlinearly with depth, and the deliberately unquoted power cord.
- **D2 — choice-primitive.** The nearest legal relative of `choose` is the controlled-unitary — coherent branching is textbook-legal; what fails is broadcasting the condition (WZ82, executed as the rephasing-covariance gap in W-B) and defaulting stability (invariance is engineered, W-B: exact for compiled targets, near the Haar mean for random programs). SEL04's quantum-data-classical-control design is the boundary the route starts from, not an oversight it corrects. The certification toll is the epoch-3 ledger re-invoiced (W-C). The Noether question stays open with its price unquoted — by law.
