# W3 — pre-arrival equivalence: correlator, not oracle

## A. before the channel: distributionally indistinguishable from a shared seed

The cache's pre-arrival deliverable is B's local answer distribution together with its (in)dependence of A's side. A classical shared-seed cache (pre-shared random bit s) delivers exactly the same object: B's data uniform, independent of everything on A's side.

- TV distance, quantum B-answer vs uniform seed bit, all axis pairs: 0.000000000000000
- TV distance, classical seed B-answer vs uniform: 0 (identical by construction)

By any LOCAL statistic, before the classical channel, the two caches are the same object.

## B. the surplus column: all 256 classical strategies vs the singlet

- the singlet at the standard axes: -2.828427124746 vs Tsirelson's 2*sqrt(2) = 2.828427124746
