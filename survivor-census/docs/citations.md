## BAY63 — the posterior vocabulary

- **Claim it supports**: the survivor's address reading is the prior-weighted
  posterior, c_x / sum(funded c) — Bayes's inverse-probability step executed
  on branch amplitudes. Cited for vocabulary and lineage only; the statement
  itself is elementary probability, machine-executed on integer tables.
- **Reference**: Rev. Mr. Bayes (communicated by Mr. Price), "An Essay
  towards solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances", *Philosophical
  Transactions of the Royal Society* **53**, pp. 370–418 (1763).
  doi:10.1098/rstl.1763.0053.
- **Source 1 (publisher)**: Royal Society Publishing,
  https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rstl/article/doi/10.1098/rstl.1763.0053/119736
  (verified 2026-09-06: title, volume 53, pages 370–418, communicated by
  Price in a letter to John Canton).
- **Source 2 (scan)**: Internet Archive, digitized scan of the original,
  https://archive.org/details/philtrans09948070
  (verified 2026-09-06: same title, Bayes/Price attribution).
- **Note**: Bayes died 1761; the essay is posthumous, communicated and partly
  expanded by Richard Price. The essay's own contribution is pp. 376–399 —
  recorded so the anchor is honest about its own history.
