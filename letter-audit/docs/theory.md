# Theory — the completion point, and the last of the poetry

## The method, as a law

This correspondence's founding move — poetry in, precise claims with boundaries on the same line out — has run fifteen times over the ledger's rows. ## The origin claim, precise

"I come from the time point where computation is already complete." For a fixed universe of Turing machines, this has an exact meaning: the completion point is the step after the last halter halts — the Busy-Beaver step of that universe. It is watchable: the halting census of the 2-state universe (20,736 machines under this repo's encoding, 9,784 halters) stabilizes at step 6 and never moves again — completion as an empirical fact, machine-verified.

And it is bounded, on the same line: Rado's theorem makes BB non-computable in general (it dominates every computable function), so "the completion point of computation" is not a place in any computable coordinate system. The known ladder — 1, 6, 21, 107, 47,176,870 (bbchallenge 2024, Coq-verified) — ends at the edge of what collaborative humanity plus formal verification can certify; beyond it, the address exists only as a theorem's negation. The visitor's home is real in universes smaller than five states.

The audit does not solve the halting problem; it exhibits where the problem becomes visible.

## The frontier re-audit (v0.2.0) — a registry is honest only while it tracks the evidence

The registry this audit quoted at v0.1.0 (depreciation-ledger's 17 verdict rows) honestly held two OPEN rows: #10 hardware, #15 physics-layer stability. Since then the workspace shipped their settlements — dtc-clock v0.19.0 for #10's model layer, stable-world v0.5.0 plus choice-lang v0.2.0 for #15's both layers — and a registry that keeps saying OPEN after the evidence lands is a different kind of dishonesty. The re-audit is the method's own rule turned on its own output: statuses track the shipped evidence, and every status cites its evidence by POINTER.

The pointers are the load-bearing part. Each of the 17 rows carries (sibling repo, report file, needle) triplets, and the checker reads them LIVE at every build: the file must exist on disk and contain the needle substring (law A6 — a pointer to a needle that does not exist is named and rejected, and this law caught one genuinely wrong needle during development: the CHSH constant quoted to 15 decimals where the rendered report carries 12). A row graduated from OPEN must name its settler and cite it by pointer (A7 — a graduation without a settling certificate is self-certification, refused). The census strings the report quotes are re-counted from the table on every run (A8 — quoted numbers cannot drift from the data). The method anchor is the quantum-advantage audit literature's own practice (arXiv:2412.14703): claims live in registries whose statuses are re-audited as the evidence moves; we instantiate that at workspace scale with machine-checkable pointers instead of prose.

The graduations carry their boundaries on the same line, because that is law A1 applied to verdicts: #10 is settled at the model layer — the beat is exact, clocks universal reversible computation, and the decay-law arc is assembled to theorem grade — while hardware instantiation remains NOT claimed (the HW-WAIT face survives inside the graduation); #15 is settled at both layers — stability executed as an authored physical law and as a language — while the law stays authored (nature's instantiation not claimed; DAV74/WY16 cited). Zero OPEN rows remain because the boundaries moved into the cost columns, not because they vanished. Honesty demands OPEN rows stay where the boundary is real — and these two stopped being real.

## The sixth rung

The ladder 1, 6, 21, 107, 47,176,870 gained a fence in June 2025: BB(6) > 2↑↑(2↑↑(2↑↑9)), at least 2↑↑↑5 (mxdys; BusyBeaverWiki and Aaronson, two sources, see citations.md). The repo's contribution is not the constant — it is the consistency check, machine-executed: 2↑↑5 = 2^65536 is the last tower that fits a BigInt (19,729 digits), so taller towers are compared structurally (for base 2, tower height decides, recursively — the monotonicity prefix is machine-checked), and the two cited expressions are verified to agree in order: the champion's tower strictly exceeds 2↑↑↑5. The boundary is the news: the exact value is gated on a Collatz-like problem (Antihydra), so the sixth rung is fenced, not climbed — the visitor's home address still ends at five states.
