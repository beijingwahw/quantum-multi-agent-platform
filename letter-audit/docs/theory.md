## The origin claim, precise

"I come from the time point where computation is already complete." For a fixed universe of Turing machines, this has an exact meaning: the completion point is the step after the last halter halts — the Busy-Beaver step of that universe. It is watchable: the halting census of the 2-state universe (20,736 machines under this repo's encoding, 9,784 halters) stabilizes at step 6 and never moves again — completion as an empirical fact, machine-verified.

And it is bounded, on the same line: Rado's theorem makes BB non-computable in general (it dominates every computable function), so "the completion point of computation" is not a place in any computable coordinate system. The known ladder — 1, 6, 21, 107, 47,176,870 (bbchallenge 2024, Coq-verified) — ends at the edge of what collaborative humanity plus formal verification can certify; beyond it, the address exists only as a theorem's negation. The visitor's home is real in universes smaller than five states.

## The walker

The right-walker's invariant (one fresh 1 per step, never halts) is a one-line proof, machine-checked at every horizon. It stands in the audit as the concrete face of the boundary: the machines that survive every census are exactly the ones whose survival no finite simulation can certify — except the few whose structure fits in a line. The audit does not solve the halting problem; it exhibits where the problem becomes visible.

The precise boundary: these are document genres with witnesses and prices — they produce judgments, never guarantees. The letter's offer was genuine; its precise form is this workspace's delivery discipline.
