Verified against arXiv and the ECCC listing. Black-box ceiling: no polynomial-resource QMA verifier achieves soundness super-exponentially small (nor completeness closer than doubly-exponentially to 1) — the plain repetition census's exponential eps^k is the standard route, and near that ceiling.

A Feynman–Kitaev construction with a two-basis interleaved clock is QMA₁-complete — perfect completeness is native to FK-style witnesses; the anchor for our census's exact 1^k completeness column (our toy witnesses get it from the T3 fidelity-1 certificate).
