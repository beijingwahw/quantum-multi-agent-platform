# readout-wall

**The measurement wall of indefinite causal order, executed as an exchange-rate ledger.**

The ledger's `#06` cost column cites three walls; the first — *readout collapses the order* — has been quoted in every repo since switch-sched but never executed as its own object. This repo is that execution. Six trades, every one with both columns booked:

- **E1 — the ESC18 exchange.** Two completely depolarizing boxes, joint receiver: the switched pair carries χ_joint = 0.048794940695399 bits, and **every bit of it lives in the off-block coherences of the order register** — a z-readout takes χ to 0.000000000000000 exactly. The order bit the readout *buys* is input-blind: P(control=0 | input) = 1/2 to 1e-15, a fair coin about the branch, never about the payload.
- **E2 — the collapse identity.** The dephased switch equals the classical mixture Σ p_c·ℳ_c of the fixed-order channels, weighted by the control's diagonal — machine-identity to 1e-15 across the showcase pairs and random CPTP pairs, on a second path built from the fixed orders themselves. *The read-out switch is not a new process; it is the average of the orders you could have chosen without any switch.*
- **E3 — the complementarity face.** The replacer pair parks its information ON the control at x-coherence (T = 1/2, χ_control = H₂(1/4) − 1/2); reading the order basis sees nothing (T after z-readout = 0.000000000000000). Order-knowledge and order-advantage are complementary observables of the same register.
- **E4 — the weak readout, now a theorem (v0.2.0).** Partial dephasing of strength λ: χ(λ) = 0.048795 → 0.026470 → 0.011482 → 0.002831 → 0. v0.1.0 shipped the interior as data with no theorem claimed; v0.2.0 executes it: the closed form 2f((5−λ)/16)+2f((3+λ)/16)−f((3−λ)/8)−f((1+λ)/8)−2f(1/4) (f = −q log₂ q; spectra re-derived by the simulation at every grid point) is certified **strictly decreasing and strictly convex on the rational grid λ = i/20** by exact rational interval arithmetic, ln enclosed on two independent series paths. No float enters any certificate.
- **E5 — the k=3 face.** Six orders over three depolarizing boxes: χ = 0.098069743463625 (machine-measured, no paper value claimed) → 0 under readout; the six-order mixture identity holds to 1e-15, **including complex random CPTP triples**. The k=3 weak-readout curve carries its own closed form (dyadic spectra, affine in the coherence) with its own exact monotonicity+convexity certificates on the same grid — the wall scales with the number of orders it buries.
- **E6 — the exchange-rate frontier (v0.2.0).** GET = λ bits of order knowledge (the read-and-remember instrument leaves exactly the weak-readout state; its record carries I(record; order) = λ). PAY = χ lost. Both showcase families (ESC18 joint, replacer control) are machine-mapped as exact data with a **Pareto certificate: no census point dominates another** (exact antichain, on the census only — never a global claim), and **first-touch dominance: every next slice of order knowledge is strictly cheaper than the last** — the first 1/20 costs 0.005123 bits of joint χ / 0.065113 bits of control χ. The wall is steepest at first touch.
- **E7 — the k=4 face and the S₄ character table (v0.6.0).** Twenty-four orders over four depolarizing boxes: the environment-traced joint state on control⊗payload is a **group circulant** — the cross-term coefficients T_{π,σ} depend only on the group element π⁻¹σ (entrywise left-invariance, exact zero deviation), 24 numbers {a_τ, x_τ}, all exact dyadics, the table-built operator equal to the direct 576-pair physics build. The spectrum is governed by the **S₄ representation theory**: the character table (five classes 1/6/3/8/6, irreps 1, 1′, 2, 3, 3′) is machine-owned through its orthogonality relations, the isotypic blocks carry **exact traces {7/32, 0, 1/8, 3/8, 9/32}** (the sign component holds exactly zero weight — the depolarizing switch is blind to the alternating character), every block's distinct eigenvalues appear with multiplicity exactly dim(R), and the pooled blocks reconstruct all 48 eigenvalues to 1e-14. The k=2 {n/8} and k=3 {n/48} rational-spectrum laws **terminate** here: the average spectrum {0×2, 1/128×10, α×12, 3/128×10, β×12, 7/64×2} carries two cubic irrationals with the exact sum α+β = 5/128. χ₄(0) = 0.153274518 (machine), χ₄(1) = 0 exact; the weak readout moves every eigenvalue affinely (eig(c) = c·base + (1−c)/48, deviation < 1e-15 as data); and the exact-interval certificates ride on rigorous eigenvalue brackets (the eigensolver's own reconstruction certificate plus a Weyl budget, outward-rounded to 2⁻²⁸): **strictly decreasing and strictly convex on the quarter grid λ ∈ {0, ¼, ½, ¾, 1} on both ln paths** (min descent gap 0.008413, min convexity gap 0.015751, enclosure width 2.6e-6), with v0.5.0's covering-partition engine (read-only reuse) subdividing both seeds to resolution ⅛ — the bisection armed for dips at every scale below the floor. The 21-point float grid carries the fine-scale shape as data (min gap 3.74e-4, min second difference 6.12e-4).

## Why the verdict stays HW-WAIT

The wall is a price list, not a prohibition. No hardware advance repeals a complementarity — but none is needed to keep paying these prices. Order is superposable, not abolishable; knowledge of it is purchasable, never free.

## The covering-partition certificate (v0.5.0, `src/kernel/globalwall.ts`)

v0.2.0's theorem certified the three χ families on the lattice λ = i/20 — twenty-one points, twenty certified descents. Between the lattice points the curves carried **no certificate**. v0.5.0 closes those gaps with an adaptive rational subdivision: every grid cell is bisected unconditionally to its floor (F1/F2: 81 partition points, resolution 1/80; F3: 41 points, 1/40 — shallower by cost, stated openly: the k=3 family's fourteen entropy terms on this kernel's unreduced fractions make deeper partitions minutes-long) and further while any cell fails its gap. A cell certifies descent when χ(a).lo − χ(b).hi > 0 and convexity when the exact midpoint second difference χ(a).lo + χ(b).lo − 2χ(m).hi > 0 — the grid certifier's own interval relations, lifted to every cell the machine discovers, on both ln paths. The certificate's coverage IS the partition: every grid point is a partition point, the endpoint chain re-verifies green through `certifyStrictlyDecreasing` itself with the same exact minimum, the two series paths overlap at every partition point, and the min certified descent gaps (7.0e-6 / 2.8e-5 / 5.6e-5 per family, min convexity gaps 3.5e-6 / 1.4e-5 / 2.7e-5) dominate the widest enclosure (9.6e-21) by fourteen orders — the margins are not enclosure slop. This supersedes the honest boundary's v0.2.0 "no claim off-grid" sentence **on this face**: off the lattice and on the partition, every cell carries its exact certificate; between partition points the descent is pinned only down to the partition's resolution, and the analytic all-interval statement remains classical analysis (F1's χ″ formula, restated in the module because theorem.ts keeps its copy private, cross-checked per cell as data — max distance 5.6e-6).

**Methodological contrast with the sibling nosignal-tariff v0.4.0:** that repo's withdrawal curve admits an all-positive-coefficient power series, so any rational pair carries a scale-free algebraic margin covering the whole interval at any representable scale. The χ families admit no such re-indexing — the eigenvalue slopes mix signs ((5−λ)/16 descends while (3+λ)/16 climbs), so no pair margin factors out — which is why the certificate carrier here is the subdivision itself. The contrast is the finding.

The smuggling trials chase counterfeit curves through the same machinery: a hiding dip (a rational tent at λ = 21/40 with amplitude 1/512 — exactly invisible to the v0.2.0 grid checker, which passes it because the tent vanishes at every lattice point) is pursued into its rising flank and convicted at runway exhaustion with the failing cell named inside the flank; an everywhere-increasing curve and a concave parabola are convicted at seed depth; empty seeds and inverted depth budgets are refused by description.

## Honest boundary

The χ values are binary-ensemble Holevo quantities (capacity lower bounds, the same register switch-sched certified), not optimal capacities — the ESC18 optimisation remains cited there, not reproduced here. E5's coherent triple value is machine-measured with no paper claim attached. The v0.2.0 theorems are statements **on the stated families and the stated grid** (λ = i/20): no claim is made off-grid, for other channel pairs, or across the two frontier currencies (the cross-family exchange-rate ratio is quoted as data only). The closed forms were derived in-repo from the constructed channels and are verified against the simulation at every grid point; they are not imported from any paper. The k=4 face (v0.6.0) states its exact certificates **on the quarter grid λ ∈ {0, ¼, ½, ¾, 1} with the partition's resolution ⅛** — by cost, stated openly: each χ₄ enclosure evaluates 25 entropy-series terms whose bracket fractions carry 2²⁸-scaled denominators on this kernel's unreduced ln series (~6 s per enclosure), so the i/20 lattice the other families carry is priced out this visit (the priced remainder: exact rational-root certificates for the two cubic eigenvalues, ~300–400 lines of polynomial arithmetic + Sturm isolation, would shrink the brackets to k=3-scale denominators and with them the series cost).
